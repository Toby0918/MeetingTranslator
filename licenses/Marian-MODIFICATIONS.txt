This is a modified model, not an unmodified upstream distribution. Weights were
fine-tuned with 1,120 AI-generated original generic bilingual examples, with 80
separate synthetic validation examples. Training ran for three epochs. Epoch 2
was selected using development evaluation and converted to CTranslate2 int8.
The tokenizer models are unchanged upstream assets. No TED transcripts were
included in the fine-tuning dataset. This project is not affiliated with TED,
Helsinki-NLP, OpenAI or DeepSeek.
